The previous modifications are [`daggersearch/daggerheart-data`](https://github.com/daggersearch/daggerheart-data),
which structured the SRD as JSON — the `core` release and the `the_void` release (the Hope &
Fear expansion's SRD portion) — plus one divergence made here: the Guardian's *Unstoppable*
is merged into a single `classFeatures` entry, described under "Data source" in the README.

## Prohibited Content

Artwork, illustrations, imagery and logos are Prohibited Content under the DPCGL and are
**never** committed to this repository. `data/*/card-art/` is git-ignored and the card renderer
degrades to a CSS-only fallback without it. The fonts in `assets/fonts/` are not Daggerheart
material: they are Cinzel, Cinzel Decorative, Montserrat and Overpass, under the SIL Open
Font License.
